D-007

Main contribution: parents' shelter (Plan A) or direct student support (Plan B)?

Needed by Fri 25 Sep · 2 weeks · @monika

Options, context and why it matters
Options
Plan A — parents' shelter, around 3,000 sq ft. One side of the sheet work alone was estimated at ₹5.4 lakh against a tentative budget of ₹3–3.5 lakh, so it is well over what the batch has unless parents contribute. Plan B — direct student support: an electric sanitary-napkin incinerator for the girls' hostel, a projector and UPS for the MP Hall, and scholarships from whatever budget remains.
Context
The school first proposed an open gym. On the 29 Aug visit the teachers advised against it — maintenance, rust, little use in rain or cold, poor feedback from other schools — and suggested a parents' shelter instead. Plan B has not been put to the school at all.
Why now
the money is the batch's single largest commitment of the year, and the school is waiting on an answer. Plan A is currently unaffordable on the numbers we have.
Route
the batch decides, then @shashikanth takes the chosen plan to the school.
Discussion
  • 2026-09-09 — @monika set out both plans and asked for views. Noted that contribution from parents was raised as a possibility and would have to be explored before Plan A could be judged affordable.
  • 2026-09-09 — Usha H.U strongly against Plan A: the school should not be asking alumni to fund buildings, the MP Hall already serves parents, and maintenance requests tend to follow. Plan B looked good to her.
  • 2026-09-09 — Bhargavi also against Plan A: parents' day is monthly and lasts a few hours, the facilities exist, and the batch should tell the school it is choosing what is most critical for students after evaluating their proposals.
